About me

TS

I'm happiest somewhere between the code and the people it's for.

I got into computer science because I like building things, and I added a business minor because I kept wanting to know who those things were for. That's why my projects usually start with a question I can't stop thinking about. Do companies lowball their earnings guidance on purpose? How does an independent producer know a sample is safe to use? For the second one, I didn't stop at the code. I went and talked to ten producers to find out what they actually needed.

These days I'm a course assistant for CS 222, helping student teams ship their first real software projects, which is easily one of my favorite parts of the week. I'm always up for a good conversation about AI, markets, or what makes a product click, so feel free to reach out.
